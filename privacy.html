Current website state

This website is a static information site. No analytics, account creation, payment processing, or registration storage is configured. The website has no signup or feedback forms. Do not send sensitive information.

Information that may be processed

A web hosting provider may process standard request data such as IP address, browser information, and requested page in server logs. The hosting provider, its retention practices, and applicable legal basis must be identified by the site owner before launch.

Future registration and communications

If registration or product-update communications are introduced, the operator should collect only information needed for the stated purpose, obtain appropriate consent, protect access, define retention and deletion periods, and provide a way to withdraw consent. No registration system is currently configured.

Payments and desktop software

No payment provider is connected, and no payment-card information is collected on this site. The current desktop application stores task, project, settings, and audit metadata in a local SQLite database. Gemini and Groq credentials are stored in the macOS Keychain. Optional cloud inference sends prompts to the selected provider; cloud use is disabled by default, and the provider's own terms govern that processing. A full product privacy notice covering retention, providers, and all data flows is still required before distribution.

Your rights and contact

Applicable privacy rights depend on the operator and your location. A privacy contact address and request process have not been configured. Add the verified contact details, jurisdiction-specific rights, and complaint process before publication.
