Why Anubis AI

Make room for more focused work.

Anubis AI begins with a simple product direction: bring specialized AI assistants and the work around them into a coherent desktop experience.

A product in active development.

Anubis AI is a local-first desktop assistant under development. Its current foundation includes a task and approval command center, an Anubis coordinator, and Thoth, a bounded software-development specialist. The goal is to make useful AI-assisted work more visible and reviewable rather than imply broad autonomy.

A locally tested Intel macOS development package exists, but it is unsigned and unnotarized and has not been released publicly. The app is not ready for general download or remote/network use.

Why the name?

The Anubis identity gives the original edition a distinctive visual anchor. The design uses restrained geometric references and warm metallic accents to complement a functional software experience, not replace it.

A long-term direction

The release architecture anticipates that additional mythology-inspired editions may someday be offered through this website. Anubis AI is the original edition. Zeus AI, Odin AI, and Anu AI are planned concepts only; no other edition is generally available.

Credibility through restraint

Additional named agents, voice features, alternate pantheon editions, and signed public releases remain planned or unverified. There are no published customer counts, testimonials, awards, partnerships, or security certifications. We will share verified product information as it becomes available.
