Policy draft

No subscriptions to cancel today.

Anubis AI does not currently accept payments or maintain paid subscriptions. This draft is not a final refund policy.

Owner and legal review required before publication. The operator, applicable jurisdiction, statutory rights, and actual billing workflow must be confirmed.

Current status

There is no active checkout or paid plan. The beta and full-product amounts shown on this website are planning targets only. No customer account or subscription-management portal is connected.

Cancellation process to define

Before paid subscriptions launch, clearly explain how a customer can cancel, when cancellation takes effect, whether access continues through a paid period, and how cancellation confirmation is provided. The method must work without contacting an unavailable support channel.

Refund policy to define

Specify how refund requests are submitted and handled, any applicable time limits or conditions, treatment of taxes, and rights required by applicable law. Do not promise a refund rule until reviewed and operationally supported.

Plan and price changes

Do not silently move beta subscribers to the full product, increase an existing subscriber’s price, or materially change included benefits. Provide clear advance disclosure and obtain appropriate consent where required.

Contact

A billing support address has not been configured. Publish a monitored contact and expected response process before enabling payments.
