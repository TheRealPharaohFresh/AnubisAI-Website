Disclosures required before checkout

Confirm the billing interval, currency, applicable taxes, renewal schedule, first charge timing, trial terms if any, accepted payment methods, billing provider, and receipts. Present the complete terms before the user confirms payment.

Subscriptions and changes

Define how users manage and cancel a subscription, how failed payments and refunds are handled, and how access changes when a subscription ends. Do not silently convert beta subscribers to another plan or raise an existing subscriber’s price. Obtain appropriate consent for any price or plan change.

Payment security

Use a properly configured payment provider and server-verified payment events. Never collect card details through an improvised form, trust a client-side success screen to grant access, or expose private keys in browser code.
